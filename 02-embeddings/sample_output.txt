PART 4: semantic search vs keyword search
================================================================================
  Query: 'How much holiday can I take?'
  keyword  cosine  document
        0   0.596  Every full-time employee receives 23 days of paid annual leave per cal
        0   0.593  Passwords must be at least 14 characters long and are rotated every 18
        0   0.550  All employees must be in the office on Tuesdays and Thursdays.
        0   0.527  The Zephyr-3 docks itself at a charging station when the battery falls
  margin between #1 and #2: 0.003

  Query: 'When should I recharge the robot?'
  keyword  cosine  document
        2   0.631  The Zephyr-3 docks itself at a charging station when the battery falls
        1   0.530  All employees must be in the office on Tuesdays and Thursdays.
        0   0.522  Passwords must be at least 14 characters long and are rotated every 18
        0   0.408  Every full-time employee receives 23 days of paid annual leave per cal
  margin between #1 and #2: 0.100

  Keyword search finds 0 useful overlapping words ('holiday' is not
  'annual leave', 'recharge' is not 'charging'). Embeddings still rank
  the right document #1. That's why RAG retrieves with embeddings.

  BUT notice the margins are small. Small embedding models squeeze all
  scores into a narrow band, so #2 can be almost as 'similar' as #1.
  That's why real systems retrieve the top-k (not just 1) and add
  hybrid search / reranking (Lesson 7).
