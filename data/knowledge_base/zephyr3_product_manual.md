# Zephyr-3 Warehouse Robot — Product Manual

## Overview
The Zephyr-3 is an autonomous mobile robot (AMR) designed by Nimbus Robotics for moving pallets in warehouses. It has a maximum payload of 1,200 kg and a top speed of 2.1 metres per second when unloaded and 1.4 metres per second when fully loaded. It navigates using a 360-degree lidar sensor combined with floor-mounted QR fiducial markers.

## Battery and Charging
The Zephyr-3 uses a 48V lithium iron phosphate (LiFePO4) battery pack with a capacity of 105 Ah. A full charge provides roughly 9 hours of typical operation. The robot docks itself automatically at a charging station when the battery falls below 18 percent. Charging from 18 percent to 90 percent takes about 55 minutes. The battery is rated for 4,000 charge cycles before its capacity drops below 80 percent.

## Safety Features
The Zephyr-3 has two red emergency stop (E-stop) buttons, one on each side of the chassis. Its safety lidar creates a protective field that slows the robot when a person comes within 2 metres and stops it completely within 0.5 metres. The robot emits a blue warning light projected on the floor 3 metres ahead of its direction of travel.

## Maintenance Schedule
Drive wheels should be inspected every 500 operating hours and replaced every 3,000 operating hours. The lidar window must be cleaned weekly with a lint-free cloth and isopropyl alcohol. A full service by a certified Nimbus technician is required every 12 months to keep the warranty valid.

## Warranty
The Zephyr-3 comes with a 3-year limited warranty covering the chassis, motors and electronics. The battery pack is covered for 2 years or 2,500 charge cycles, whichever comes first. Damage caused by operating the robot outside its rated payload is not covered.
