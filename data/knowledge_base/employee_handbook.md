# Nimbus Robotics — Employee Handbook

## Annual Leave
Every full-time employee at Nimbus Robotics receives 23 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Part-time employees receive leave pro-rated to their contracted hours. Up to 5 unused days may be carried over into the next year, but carried-over days expire on March 31. Leave requests of more than 10 consecutive days must be approved by a department head at least 6 weeks in advance.

## Sick Leave
Employees may take up to 12 days of paid sick leave per year. For absences longer than 3 consecutive days, a medical certificate must be uploaded to the HR portal "PeopleDesk" within 48 hours of returning to work.

## Remote Work Policy
Nimbus follows a "Core Days" hybrid model. All employees must be in the office on Tuesdays and Thursdays, which are the Core Days. The remaining days may be worked remotely. Employees working remotely must be reachable on Slack between 10:00 and 16:00 local time. Fully remote arrangements require written approval from the VP of People, Dana Okafor.

## Home Office Stipend
Each employee receives a one-time home office stipend of 750 euros, claimable within the first 90 days of employment. Receipts must be submitted through PeopleDesk. The stipend covers desks, chairs, monitors and lighting, but not personal computers, which are provided by IT.

## Parental Leave
Primary caregivers receive 20 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Secondary caregivers receive 8 weeks of fully paid parental leave. Parental leave can be taken in up to three separate blocks within 18 months of the child's birth or adoption.
