# Nimbus Robotics — IT Help Desk FAQ

## How do I reset my password?
Go to https://id.nimbus.internal and click "Forgot password". Passwords must be at least 14 characters long and are rotated every 180 days. You cannot reuse any of your last 6 passwords.

## Which laptop will I get?
Engineers receive a 16-inch laptop with 64 GB of RAM. All other staff receive a 14-inch laptop with 32 GB of RAM. Laptops are refreshed every 3 years.

## How do I connect to the VPN?
Nimbus uses the "NimbusConnect" VPN client. VPN is required to access the Fleet Console and internal wikis from outside the office. Multi-factor authentication through the Authenticator app is mandatory.

## How do I report a security incident?
Email security@nimbus.internal or post in the #sec-incidents Slack channel immediately. Do not try to investigate suspected phishing emails yourself. The security on-call engineer will respond within 15 minutes during business hours and within 1 hour outside business hours.

## Can I install my own software?
Software from the Nimbus Self-Service catalog can be installed without approval. Anything else requires a ticket in the IT portal and approval from the Security team, which usually takes 2 business days.
