# Nimbus Robotics — Company Overview

## History
Nimbus Robotics was founded in 2017 in Rotterdam, Netherlands, by Ines Varga and Tomasz Brenner. Its first product, the Zephyr-1, shipped in 2019. The Zephyr-3, released in 2024, is the company's current flagship robot.

## Offices
Nimbus has three offices: headquarters in Rotterdam, an engineering hub in Kraków, Poland, and a sales office in Austin, Texas. The Kraków office hosts the lidar and perception team.

## Mission and Values
The mission of Nimbus Robotics is "to make every warehouse safer and calmer." The company's three values are Safety First, Build Boringly Reliable Things, and Share What You Learn.

## Customers
Nimbus serves more than 140 logistics customers across 11 countries. Its largest customer operates 380 Zephyr robots across 9 distribution centres.
