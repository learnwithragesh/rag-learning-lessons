PART 2: why not just embed the whole document?
================================================================================
  similarity(query, WHOLE handbook)        = 0.681
  similarity(query, 'Home Office' chunk)   = 0.840
  -> one vector for 5 topics is a blurry average of all of them.
     A focused chunk matches more strongly, and it's cheaper to put
     in the prompt.

PART 3: contextual headers (the fix for Lesson 1, Q2)
================================================================================
  WITHOUT header: "The lidar's measured distances disagree with known fiducial marker positions by more than "...
  WITH header   : "Zephyr-3 Error Codes and Troubleshooting > E-417: Lidar Calibration Drift\nThe lidar's meas"...
  Without the header, the chunk text never says 'E-417' or 'Zephyr-3'
  (that was in the heading line, which chunking removed)!

  similarity(query, chunk WITHOUT header) = 0.518
  similarity(query, chunk WITH header)    = 0.584
  -> the header gives both better retrieval AND gives the LLM the
     context it needs to answer. (In Lesson 1 it refused for lack of it.)
